SPOONFUL, INC. — CUSTOMER SUCCESS STRATEGY
Document owner: VP of Customer Success
Classification: Internal
Last reviewed: January 2026

STRATEGIC INTENT
Spoonful's customer success strategy is built on a simple premise: retained and
expanding customers drive more durable revenue than new logos alone. The
strategy focuses on reducing churn, increasing product adoption, and growing
revenue within the existing customer base.

CUSTOMER SEGMENTATION
Customers are segmented into three tiers. Enterprise accounts receive a named
Customer Success Manager (CSM) and a quarterly business review. Mid-market
accounts share a pooled CSM team and receive a semi-annual review. Self-serve
accounts are supported through in-product guidance, webinars, and a help center.
Investment is concentrated where revenue and churn risk are highest.

HEALTH SCORING
Every account carries a health score based on product usage, support case
history, satisfaction score, and payment timeliness. Accounts with declining
usage or a falling satisfaction score are flagged as at-risk and enter a defined
recovery playbook. A satisfaction score below 3.0 is treated as a strong churn
predictor and triggers immediate CSM outreach.

ONBOARDING
The first 90 days determine long-term retention. Every new customer follows a
structured onboarding plan with defined success milestones. Customers who reach
their first value milestone within 30 days churn at a markedly lower rate, so
onboarding speed is a tracked metric.

REDUCING CHURN
Churn is managed proactively rather than reactively. The team monitors leading
indicators — login frequency, feature adoption, and open complaint volume —
rather than waiting for a renewal date. Industries with historically higher
churn receive additional touchpoints and tailored adoption content.

EXPANSION
Expansion revenue comes from seat growth and upsell into additional product
lines. CSMs identify expansion opportunities during business reviews, and
expansion is only pursued once an account is healthy. Pushing expansion on an
unhealthy account is explicitly discouraged, as it accelerates churn.

ALIGNMENT WITH SALES AND PRODUCT
Customer Success shares churn-reason and complaint data with Sales to improve
qualification and with Product to prioritize the roadmap. Regional sales
performance is reviewed jointly so that strong-selling regions receive the
success capacity needed to retain what they win.
