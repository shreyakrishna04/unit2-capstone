SPOONFUL, INC. — EMPLOYEE ENGAGEMENT AND SATISFACTION POLICY
Document owner: Chief People Officer
Classification: Internal
Last reviewed: December 2025

PURPOSE
Spoonful treats employee engagement as a core business metric. This policy
describes how the company measures satisfaction, acts on the results, and
supports retention across all departments.

MEASURING SATISFACTION
Spoonful runs a company-wide engagement survey twice a year and a short pulse
survey each month. Survey results are reported on a satisfaction score from 1 to
5. Scores are reviewed by department so that leaders can see where engagement is
strong and where it needs attention. Results are shared transparently with the
whole company within two weeks of each survey closing.

BENCHMARKS AND TARGETS
The company targets an overall satisfaction score of 4.0 or higher. Industry
benchmarks for comparable B2B software firms typically fall between 3.6 and 3.9,
so Spoonful aims to stay above the sector norm. Any department scoring below 3.5
triggers a mandatory action plan owned by that department's leader and reviewed
by the People team within 30 days.

DRIVERS OF ENGAGEMENT
Internal analysis consistently links higher satisfaction to three factors:
clarity of role and expectations, quality of the direct manager relationship,
and opportunities for growth. The company invests in manager training because
the manager relationship is the single largest driver of team-level scores.

TENURE AND RETENTION
Engagement tends to dip around the 18-to-24-month tenure mark, when early
growth has slowed but senior opportunities may not yet be available. To address
this, Spoonful offers internal mobility postings, a formal mentorship program,
and development budgets for every employee. Retention is tracked alongside
satisfaction, as the two move together.

ACTING ON RESULTS
Every manager is expected to hold a team discussion of survey results and agree
at least one concrete action. The People team tracks whether committed actions
are completed. Engagement outcomes are a factor in leadership performance
reviews.

WELLBEING AND FLEXIBILITY
Spoonful provides flexible working arrangements, a wellbeing allowance, and
protected no-meeting focus time. These programs are reviewed annually against
survey feedback to confirm they still meet employee needs.
