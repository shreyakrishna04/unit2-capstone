SPOONFUL, INC. — CUSTOMER COMPLAINT HANDLING PROCEDURE
Document owner: Director of Customer Success
Classification: Internal
Last reviewed: February 2026

PURPOSE
This procedure ensures every customer complaint is captured, acknowledged,
resolved, and learned from. It applies to all customer-facing teams, including
Support, Customer Success, and Sales.

INTAKE CHANNELS
Complaints may arrive through the support portal, email, in-app feedback, or
directly to an account manager. Regardless of channel, every complaint is logged
in the support system as a case with a severity rating. No complaint is handled
informally or left unlogged.

SEVERITY LEVELS
Complaints are triaged into three severity levels. Severity 1 (critical) covers
service outages or data issues affecting production use. Severity 2 (major)
covers significant functionality problems with a workaround. Severity 3 (minor)
covers general dissatisfaction, usability friction, or feature requests.

ACKNOWLEDGEMENT AND RESOLUTION TARGETS
Severity 1 complaints are acknowledged within one hour and worked continuously
until resolved, with a target resolution of four hours. Severity 2 complaints
are acknowledged within four business hours with a target resolution of two
business days. Severity 3 complaints are acknowledged within one business day
with a target resolution of five business days.

ESCALATION
If a complaint cannot be resolved within its target, it is escalated to the team
lead, and for Severity 1, to the Director of Customer Success and the engineering
on-call. Any complaint from an enterprise-tier customer is automatically copied
to the assigned account manager.

CLOSING THE LOOP
Before a case is closed, the customer confirms the resolution is satisfactory.
The owner records the root cause and whether the issue was a product defect, a
process gap, or a misunderstanding. Cases closed without customer confirmation
are reopened automatically after follow-up.

LEARNING FROM COMPLAINTS
Complaint data is reviewed monthly by Customer Success leadership. Recurring
themes are shared with Product and Engineering to drive fixes. A rise in
Severity 1 or Severity 2 volume for any product line is treated as an early
warning signal for churn risk and triggers a dedicated review.
