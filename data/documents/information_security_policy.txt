SPOONFUL, INC. — INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
Document owner: Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)
Classification: Internal
Last reviewed: January 2026

PURPOSE
This policy defines how Spoonful protects the confidentiality, integrity, and
availability of company and customer information. It applies to all employees,
contractors, and third parties who access Spoonful systems or data.

ACCESS CONTROL
Access to systems is granted on a least-privilege basis. Every employee receives
only the access required for their role. Access is reviewed quarterly by the
system owner and the employee's manager. Privileged (administrator) access
requires CISO approval and is reviewed monthly. All access is revoked within
four hours of an employee's departure.

AUTHENTICATION
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all corporate accounts,
including email, source control, cloud consoles, and the customer production
environment. Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are managed through
the approved enterprise password manager. Shared accounts are prohibited.

DATA CLASSIFICATION
Spoonful classifies data into four tiers: Public, Internal, Confidential, and
Restricted. Customer data and personal data are always treated as Restricted.
Restricted data must be encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest
(AES-256). Restricted data may never be copied to personal devices or
unapproved cloud storage.

INCIDENT RESPONSE
Any suspected security incident must be reported to the Security Operations
Center within one hour of discovery via the #security-incident channel or the
security hotline. The incident response team follows a four-phase process:
identify, contain, eradicate, recover. A post-incident review is completed
within five business days for every confirmed incident. Customers affected by a
confirmed data breach are notified within 72 hours in line with regulatory
requirements.

ACCEPTABLE USE
Company systems are provided for business use. Employees must not disable
security controls, install unapproved software, or attempt to access data
outside their authorization. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to
and including termination.

THIRD-PARTY RISK
All vendors handling Spoonful data must complete a security assessment before
onboarding and annually thereafter. Vendors handling Restricted data must
provide a current SOC 2 Type II report.

POLICY REVIEW
This policy is reviewed at least annually by the CISO and approved by the
executive risk committee. Exceptions require documented CISO approval with a
defined expiry date.
